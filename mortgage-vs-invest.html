Net worth on the chart is portfolio value minus remaining mortgage balance, so it shows the three paths on equal footing at every point in time. Mortgage interest compounds monthly; stock returns compound monthly at the equivalent of the annual rate. Total out-of-pocket spending is identical in all three scenarios, and the mortgage is fully paid in all three by the end, so the final value is the whole comparison. Not included: CPI-linked tracks, early-repayment fees, and the fact that stock returns vary from year to year.
