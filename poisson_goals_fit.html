Goals scored by a team in one game are (nearly) Poisson

Take every team-innings from the major national-team finals tournaments and count how many goals it scored. The histogram of those counts is tracked almost perfectly by a Poisson distribution whose single parameter λ is just the average goals per team per game. That is the whole premise of the scoreline model: predict each team's λ, then read off the scoreline probabilities.

All major finals (obs.) FIFA World Cup only (obs.) Poisson fit (λ = mean)
