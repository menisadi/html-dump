Wide View

The camera films what's in front of you, and the image is squeezed into a circle in the middle of the screen, like a lens that fits a wide field into a narrow field of vision.

Hold the phone at arm's length and look at the center of the circle. You can switch between four mappings and adjust every parameter.

If the camera doesn't open: take a photo or video at 0.5x zoom (the ultrawide lens) and choose it here. Then set "Camera field" to about 110°.

Mapping

Roughly the size of the remaining field of vision. The sharp zone keeps this scale.
Share of the circle's radius left undistorted. Everything outside it is compressed periphery.
Along the long side of the image. About 70° for the main camera, 105–115° for the ultrawide.
